# ImperiumMC Brand Guidelines

We welcome people using and adapting our branding to discuss, promote, review, document, and create content about
ImperiumMC. These guidelines explain our brand preferences and the trademark permission we offer separately from the
copyright license. The logo files, name, and colors are listed in the [README](README.md).

specifically marked otherwise. That license permits copying, adaptation, and commercial use, subject to its terms. It
does not license trademark rights.

These guidelines do not narrow the copyright permissions granted by CC BY 4.0, impose additional copyright restrictions,
or override applicable legal exceptions. References to unauthorized brand use concern trademark rights where those
rights apply.

## Permitted brand use

You may display the ImperiumMC name and logos to truthfully identify, discuss, review, promote, or link to ImperiumMC.
This includes:

- Videos, livestreams, thumbnails, and social media posts.
- Server listings, reviews, articles, news, and commentary.
- Wikis, guides, tutorials, and documentation.
- Fan art, community projects, and ImperiumMC-related event graphics.
- Truthful statements about compatibility, integrations, or authorized relationships.

Monetized content may use the branding for these purposes. Permission is not limited to favorable coverage: criticism
and commentary are welcome on the same terms.

Make your own identity and any unofficial status clear where confusion is reasonably likely. Do not describe a
relationship as official unless ImperiumMC has actually authorized it.

## Attribution and website link

For sharing or public display under CC BY 4.0, identify **ImperiumMC** as the attribution party and retain the supplied
website URI, license reference, and relevant notices in a manner reasonable for the medium. Include:

- **Credit:** ImperiumMC.
- **Website:** https://imperium-mc.net.
- **License:** https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/.
- **Changes:** Indicate whether you modified the asset and retain relevant previous modification notices.

A clickable website link should accompany logo use when the medium supports one. A video description, stream panel,
article caption, listing page, or credits section can provide the link; it does not have to be embedded inside the
artwork. For non-clickable media, display `imperium-mc.net` and the license URL in accompanying credits or text.

Example:

> [ImperiumMC](https://imperium-mc.net) branding, licensed
> under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Unmodified.

For an adaptation, replace “Unmodified” with a description such as “Modified by \[Creator\]: seasonal effects added.”

Attribution must not falsely imply endorsement. These attribution directions implement the supplied credit information
under CC BY 4.0; the license governs the precise requirements and reasonable manner of compliance. Uses independently
permitted by law do not require permission under this policy.

## Logo modifications and fan content

You may modify the logo for seasonal designs, holiday themes, events, animations, thumbnails, backgrounds, borders,
cropping, resizing, recoloring, and other creative adaptations.

When sharing modified artwork, indicate that changes were made. If your design could be mistaken for an official asset
or announcement, clearly identify it as an unofficial or fan-created adaptation. Only assets issued or approved by
ImperiumMC should be described as official ImperiumMC branding.

We recommend preserving the logo's proportions and readability when using the standard official asset. These are visual
preferences, not additional copyright license conditions.

Creators retain any rights they have in their original contributions, subject to the rights in incorporated material and
the applicable license.

## Impersonation and confusing brand use

We do not grant trademark permission to:

- Impersonate ImperiumMC or its staff.
- Brand another server, product, business, or service with our identifiers in a way likely to confuse people about its
  source, sponsorship, or affiliation.
- Falsely claim that ImperiumMC operates, sponsors, endorses, or officially partners with your project.
- Create misleading domains or accounts that appear to be official ImperiumMC channels.
- Register our brand identifiers as your own in a manner that infringes applicable rights.

Do not claim authorship of the original ImperiumMC artwork. An adaptation must credit the original and identify your
changes under the copyright license.

Other Minecraft servers may refer to ImperiumMC in truthful comparisons, commentary, news, compatibility information, or
descriptions of genuine relationships. Make the distinction between the servers clear where needed to avoid confusion.
